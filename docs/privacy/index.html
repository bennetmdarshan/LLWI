PRIVACY & PHOTOGRAPHS

A little care
goes a long way.

Information about this website, your enquiry and the photographs you see here.

Browsing this website

This website has no enquiry form, payment checkout or health-information form. It does not include custom advertising trackers or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site.

Starting a conversation

Contact buttons open WhatsApp with a short, prefilled message. Nothing is sent automatically. You decide whether to edit and send the message. WhatsApp handles that conversation under its own terms and privacy practices. Share only the information you want to discuss.

Photos and personal stories

The photographs on this site are from the LLWI community album. Captions describe the scene without identifying anyone’s health status. If you have a question about a photograph, its use, or a correction or removal request, contact Anurati.

Motion preference

The motion control changes the animation for your current visit. The site also respects your device’s reduced-motion setting. It does not save this preference to a tracking profile.

Trip and booking information

Before sharing personal details or booking, ask Anurati how booking information will be used, stored and shared. Trip-specific arrangements should be confirmed directly.
