How are trips planned around T1D-related needs?+

Talk to Anurati about the specific itinerary, meal timings, activity demands, storage arrangements and support available. These details should be confirmed for each trip. Community support does not replace your personal diabetes management or medical care.

What is included in the price?+

Inclusions, exclusions, accommodation, transport and payment terms vary by trip. Request the full written details from Anurati before making a payment.

Are there cancellation or refund policies?+

Ask Anurati for the cancellation, refund and any transfer terms for your specific trip before booking. No universal cancellation or refund policy is listed on this site.
