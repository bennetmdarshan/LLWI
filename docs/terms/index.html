A few things to know before you enquire or plan a trip.

About the website

This is an introduction to Living Life With Insulin and its community. Photo albums show past moments; they are not offers to book a departure. Journal reflections are general editorial content, not traveller testimonials.

Before you book

Confirm the itinerary, dates, group size, price, inclusions, exclusions, accommodation, transport, payment terms and eligibility with Anurati. Request the trip’s written cancellation, refund and transfer policies before making any payment. This website does not process bookings or payments.

Individual needs and support

Support arrangements, accessibility, meals, emergency plans and any insurance or medical personnel must be confirmed for the specific trip. Community and group planning do not replace individual diabetes management or professional medical care. This website does not provide medical advice.

Questions or corrections

If something is unclear, or you spot information that needs correcting, please get in touch with Anurati.
