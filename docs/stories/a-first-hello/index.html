FIRST-TIME ADVENTURES

The first step can be a hello.

You don’t need to have the whole journey figured out to start a conversation.

A JOURNAL REFLECTION · 2 MIN READ

Curiosity is a good starting point.

Maybe you know exactly where you want to go. Maybe you’re still wondering what travelling with a new group would feel like. Both are good places to begin. A first message can be as simple as asking what’s coming up.

Ask the ordinary questions.

Who is organising the trip? How long is it? What will the days look like? What is included? Practical details make an unfamiliar experience easier to picture. There is room to ask about your own needs, too.

Take your time deciding.

An enquiry is a conversation, not a commitment. Get to know the plans and the people behind them. Think about what you want from the experience. You get to decide what feels right for you.
